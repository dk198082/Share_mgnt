---
name: Certificate PDF policy
description: User-approved requirements for blank templates, completed certificates, and historical authenticity.
---
Administrators manage separate blank editable Common and Preferred certificate templates in Settings. The user explicitly requested converting each supplied issued example into a blank template, not retaining the sample's holder, date, number, or old officer signatures.

**Why:** Common and Preferred examples have different legal terms, including different par values. Settings market share prices must not replace legal par values.

**How to apply:** Preserve class-specific legal text and reverse-side terms when updating templates. Remove variable personal details using actual redaction, not a white overlay.

Completed issuance and transfer certificates must include the signatures recorded through the approved Settings-based sign-off workflow. Uploaded signature images alone are not approval. Do not infer titles or substitute the old CEO/CFO signatures.

**Why:** The user explicitly selected inclusion of recorded approval signatures and previously replaced role-based approval with named Settings signers.

**How to apply:** Retain the completed certificate PDF alongside the transaction and make it viewable in shareholder history. For transfers, make the same retained PDF discoverable from both parties. Preserve earlier documents when templates change; never regenerate old completed certificates using today's holder information or signatures.

Blank derivatives of the supplied issued PDFs must also remove embedded attachments, not just redact visible details.

**Why:** The supplied samples included embedded files that survived visible redaction and caused the application’s template security validator to reject re-uploaded blanks.

**How to apply:** Strip attachments and their PDF name-tree references, garbage-collect orphaned content, and run the application’s template validator before delivering a blank. Do not weaken the validator to accept the original embedded files.
